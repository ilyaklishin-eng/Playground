Selected work by Ilia Klishin

Ilia Klishin is a Russian journalist, media manager, and media consultant. His work has traced the rise of Russian digital propaganda, the pressure placed on independent media, the political aftermath of the 2011–2012 protest movement, and the changing civic life of Russian-speaking communities in exile.

This archive is organized as a set of editorial routes rather than a simple timeline: information war, Russian politics, media under pressure, and exile. Together, these routes show how political power captures language, platforms, institutions, and public imagination.

Information War

These pieces follow Russian propaganda as a working system: troll farms, bot networks, platform pressure, and the shift from crude online interference to adaptive campaigns across Western and youth-facing platforms.
